[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
This game takes place in a Japanese garden with moss and trees. In the garden there is a shishi-odoshi. A shishi-odoshi is a bamboo tube that fills with water. When it is empty, it falls back and hits a stone. It makes the sound "kakoon".
Tap the screen at the moment the bamboo hits the stone. If your timing is perfect, you get a PERFECT.

■ HOW THE SHISHI-ODOSHI MOVES
• Water flows into the bamboo tube. The tube slowly tips down.
• The water pours out. The empty tube falls back and hits the stone.
• The water goes into a stone bowl. Then it flows in a small stream to a pond with fish.

■ HOW TO PLAY
• A ring moves toward the stone. Tap when the ring touches the ring on the stone.
• There are four results: PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD and MISS.
• If you succeed many times in a row, you get more points. The bonus can be up to 3 times.
• The game ends after three MISS results.

■ PERFECT EFFECTS
• You see a gold light, a column of light and many cherry blossom petals.
• The camera moves closer, and you hear bells.

■ THE GARDEN CHANGES
• Rain: the water flows faster.
• Wind: the bamboo moves in a different way.
• Night: the garden is dark. The lanterns are on.
• Tube size: the tube becomes thick or thin.
• Two tubes: a second shishi-odoshi appears.
• At higher levels, these changes come together.

■ THE GARDEN
• There is a tea house. A cat sleeps on the veranda.
• There is a cherry tree, a pine tree, a stone lantern, a rock garden, bamboo and maple trees.
• The camera moves around the garden. You see the garden from many angles.

■ SOUND
• You hear the "kakoon" sound, water, rain and wind.
• Calm music plays on a Japanese string instrument.

■ OTHER FEATURES
• You can get ranks, from "Novice Gardener" to "Legendary Gardener".
• You can share your score with friends.
• Practice mode: you can choose the garden changes.
• After the third MISS, you can watch an ad to remove one MISS and continue. You can do this two times in one game.
• You can play offline. Ads and sharing need the internet.
• Languages: English and Japanese.
