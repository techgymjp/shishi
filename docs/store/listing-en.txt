[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
In a mossy Japanese garden, a shishi-odoshi rings out: "kakoon!"
Tap the screen at the very moment the bamboo strikes the stone. Hit it right on, and it's a PERFECT.
A one-tap timing game to sharpen your rhythm while the sounds and scenery help you unwind.

■ A REAL SHISHI-ODOSHI
• Water from the bamboo spout fills the tube, and its mouth slowly sinks
• Once the water spills out, the light tube swings back and strikes the stone
• The water flows into a stone basin, over the rim, down a little stream and into a koi pond

■ TAP ON THE "KAKOON!"
• A ring shrinks toward the stone. When it meets the ring on the stone, that's a PERFECT
• Four ratings: PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD and MISS
• Keep hitting to raise your combo multiplier, up to ×3
• Three MISSes and the game is over

■ PERFECTS THAT SHINE
• A golden flash, a pillar of light rising from the stone and a burst of cherry petals
• The camera pushes in as bells climb a Japanese scale
• More petals with every PERFECT in a row

■ THE GARDEN CHANGES AS YOU LEVEL UP
• Rain: the water flows faster
• Wind: the bamboo moves a little differently, ferns sway and leaves drift by
• Night: a moonlit garden, glowing lanterns and shoji, and fireflies
• Tube size: thick tubes and thin tubes
• Two shishi-odoshi: a second one appears, and they take turns
• From level 7, these come in combinations

■ A JAPANESE GARDEN TO EXPLORE
• A mossy forest, a tea house with a cat napping on the veranda, a weeping cherry and a black pine
• A raked-gravel rock garden, a stone lantern, stepping stones, a bamboo grove and maples
• The camera slowly circles the garden, from ground level to a high view, so every game looks different

■ MADE FOR YOUR EARS
• The "kakoon" itself, water filling the bamboo, rain and wind
• A slow, gentle soundtrack on the taishōgoto (a Japanese zither), with a new melody every time

■ AND MORE
• Ranks from "Novice Gardener" to "Legendary Gardener", plus your play records
• Share your score and rank with friends
• Practice mode to try any garden effect from the start
• After your third MISS, watch an ad to undo one MISS and keep playing (up to 2 times per game)
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese

Now, listen closely... kakoon!
